Images Video frames AI-ready

1. Upload media

Upload content you own or have permission to edit.

Pause the video on the frame you want to edit. This version exports individual frames, not complete videos.

Drag over the image to select the area to clean. On video, pause first.

2. Cleanup methods

AI inpainting reconstructs a selected area using a trained model. Configure a compatible backend below to enable it.

3. AI inpainting connection

Enter the URL of your deployed image-inpainting API. Do not place secret API keys in this public website.

Expected request: POST multipart/form-data with fields image and mask. The response must be the edited image. White mask pixels indicate areas to reconstruct.

AI quality depends on the model and image. Reconstructed areas may differ from the original background.
